# Title Naming the Decision

> **Navigation**: [Decisions](./README.md) | [Documentation Root](../README.md)

Copy this file to a name for the subject, such as `STORAGE_FORMAT.md`, and fill in each
section. Keep a decision once it is superseded, and say what replaced it.

**Status.** Proposed, Decided, or Superseded by a named decision.
**Date.** YYYY-MM-DD, in Coordinated Universal Time.
**Decided by.** Who made the decision, such as the operator or a named line.

## Context

The problem, the constraints, and what forces a decision now. Mark each statement as a
fact, an inference or a hypothesis.

## Options

1. **First option.** What it is, and its consequences.
2. **Second option.** What it is, and its consequences.

## Decision

The option chosen, and the reason for it.

## Consequences

What follows from the decision, including costs, risks and what would cause it to be
revisited.
